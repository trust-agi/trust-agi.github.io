Yanjun Zhang is currently a Senior Lecturer with the School of Information and Communication Technology, Griffith University, Australia. Prior to this, she was with the School of Computer Science, University of Technology Sydney from 2023 to 2026; and School of Information Technology, Deakin University, from 2022 to 2023. She received her Ph.D. degree in Computer Science from the University of Queensland in 2021.

Yanjun’s research interest is mainly in trustworthy AI (adversarial /backdoor/poisoning/privacy attacks & defenses). Her research has been published in top-tier journals and conferences, including IEEE TIFS, IEEE TDSC, IEEE S&P,  NDSS, ICML, CVPR, AAAI, etc.
